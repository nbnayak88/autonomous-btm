Job Component Why this one Status
Load, transform and model P2P data Process Intelligence (ETL, templated P2P transformations, object-centric model) Core mining engine; templates shorten the build Available

Target process with risks and controls Process Manager BPMN model that conformance compares against Available
Share with auditors and owners Collaboration Hub Read access without modeller licences Available
Route, approve, track Process Governance Workflow for deviations and model lifecycle Available
Ask questions in plain language Joule in the suite; Process Consulting Agent Assistive analysis; grounded in the data Available (AI allowance applies)
Suggest drivers of a deviation AI-assisted root-cause analysis Speeds investigation; analyst confirms Beta
Agents that monitor and propose Agentic process and risk mining Future direction Roadmap

Not in the first release: Journey Modeler, Transformation Manager and Explorer. They are useful for customer-journey and transformation work but do not serve the audit test chain.
